Kinso — miss a day, keep the chain

A habit tracker built around one rule: missing a single day shouldn't erase 41 days of work. You get 48 hours to repair the day with a Shield you earn by keeping a 7-day streak — or by watching a short video. Two rescues a week, never two days in a row, and repaired days are drawn differently. Kinso doesn't prettify your stats.

  • Get it on Google Play

    Free, ad-supported, no account. 12 languages.

  • App Store: in review

    The iOS build is waiting for Apple. Support page has the details.

What's missing today: no widget yet, no cloud sync (JSON export instead), no editing of days older than 48 hours. Habit data never leaves the device; three anonymous events and crash reports can be switched off in Settings.
